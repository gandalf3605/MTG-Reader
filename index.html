About this reader

    
Hold a Magic card upright with its name at the top. The reader finds the name, looks the card up, and reads the game text aloud: name, type, mana cost, power and toughness, and rules. Tap the screen to hear it again.

    
Camera pictures stay on your phone. Only the card name is sent to Scryfall to look up the card.

    
It reads what is on the card in your camera. Printing details like set and year are left out on purpose, because the rules text is the same on every printing.

    
It finds the card in the picture, straightens it, reads the name bar, and double-checks the artwork against a public fingerprint list. The first time you open it, it needs the internet to download the reader and the card list (about 35 MB). After that, cards you have already scanned work without a connection.
